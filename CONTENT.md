## How it works

- H2: **The coding-agent loop, applied to physical products.**
- Intro keeps the end-to-end system aspirational.

1. **Describe** — turn plain language, sketches, or references into a clear specification.
2. **Generate** — develop the engineering design and production files.
3. **Test + repair** — check geometry, physics, and manufacturability and revise failures.
4. **Deliver** — qualified manufacturers quote and build; the outcome closes the loop.
