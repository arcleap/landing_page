# ArcLeap — Brand Spec

**Status:** Parent-company iteration for protected review
**Updated:** 2026-08-05

## Identity

ArcLeap is an independent AI company building a family of products at the intersection of intelligence and the physical world.

- ArcLeap is the company, not a single product or workflow.
- The company may build for different people, markets, and scales without defining itself as B2B or B2C.
- Wishlist is one product in the portfolio; it is not the definition of ArcLeap.

## Voice

1. Calm, ambitious, and plain-spoken.
2. Broad about the company while staying concrete enough to mean something.
3. Aspirational language must read as direction, not unsupported present capability.
4. Products appear as examples of the thesis, never as the whole company.

Avoid narrow part categories, workflow diagrams, internal strategy, inflated technical claims, hype, emojis, and competitor callouts.

## Visual direction

The identity evolves the existing `arcleap.ai` editorial character into a broader parent-company system.

- Near-black ground rather than an engineering-blue interface.
- Warm off-white reading color with amber and quiet violet accents.
- Abstract arcs, signals, fields, and emerging forms that can represent multiple products and scales.
- Editorial typography and generous negative space.
- No CAD drawings, dimension marks, robot arms, factory stock photography, glowing brains, or speculative product renders.

## Type and layout

- Fraunces for expressive company-level headlines.
- Inter for body copy and JetBrains Mono for navigation and labels.
- Twelve-column desktop grid, max width 1200px.
- One narrative column on small screens.
- Motion is restrained, decorative, and removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Interaction and accessibility

- The hero CTA moves to the company direction rather than requesting a specific product action.
- Navigation uses Direction, Work, Company, and Contact anchors.
- Focus uses a visible 2px amber outline with offset.
- Decorative artwork is hidden from assistive technology and text maintains WCAG AA contrast.

## Release boundaries

- Protected Preview carries an explicit review banner and noindex controls.
- Production never inherits Preview noindex headers.
- Wishlist may be named at high altitude; private mechanics and launch claims remain excluded.
- JinMiao Signals and `jinmiao.ai` remain separate from ArcLeap branding and navigation.
